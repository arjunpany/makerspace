Yosemite Gunna Remix

A remix with a melody close to "Yosemite" by Travis Scott and Gunna, with the sample flipped and re-energized in the second half.

Timeframe
August 2025
Genre
Hip-Hop
Category

How it was made

The melody is very similar to "Yosemite" by Travis Scott and Gunna. I took the sample, added extra melodies on top of it, and kept the drums simple trap in the first part. In the second part, I flipped the sample and brought in more energetic drums to shift the feel.
