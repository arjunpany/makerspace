Dual Threat

A jazz-hip-hop fusion piece written at a Stanford CCRMA workshop, blending a piano MIDI composition with live guitar, bass, violin, sax, and vocals from fellow workshop musicians.

How it was made

Dual Threat was written and produced during a Stanford CCRMA workshop as a piece meant to blend jazz and hip-hop into one track. I started by composing a piano MIDI part as the foundation, then layered it with guitar, bass, violin, and sax performed live by other musicians at the workshop.

For the vocals, a female singer performed the jazz section, and a male vocalist handled the interlude and the first rap verse — splitting the track between its jazz and hip-hop halves while keeping both sides connected through the same piano foundation.
