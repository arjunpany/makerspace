21 Savage Acapella Beat

A hip-hop beat built around a snapped 21 Savage acapella, with a catchy repetitive melody grown from a single sample hit and layered trap drums.

Timeframe
April 2022
Genre
Hip-Hop, Trap
Category
Beat Making, Sampling

How it was made

I took a 21 Savage acapella and snapped it onto a beat I built from scratch. The instrumental started from a single, simple sample hit, which I turned into a catchy, repetitive melody by looping and building around it. From there I layered in trap drums to give it the low end and rhythm to sit under the vocal.
