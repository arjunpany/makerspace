The Weeknd x Travis Scott Beat

A two-part beat: a Weeknd-style first half built for singing, transitioning through a water sample into a hard-hitting Travis Scott-style hip-hop second half.

How it was made

This one is split into two parts. The first half is styled after The Weeknd, with simple drums that leave room for singing over the top. It transitions into the second half through a water sample, which leads into a harder-hitting, Travis Scott-style hip-hop beat with heavier drums.
