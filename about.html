About me

I'm a high school student, but if you boil everything on this site down to one thing, it's this: I'm creative, period. Engineering and music look like different worlds, but for me they're the same instinct pointed in different directions — wiring up a competition robot or writing firmware scratches the same itch as flipping a sample into a beat or writing a melody. Both are about taking raw material, figuring out how the pieces fit, and building something that wasn't there before. This site is a log of both halves of that: the devices and DIY builds on the Engineering page, and the beats and songs on the Music page.

Skills & tools

Engineering: Arduino / Raspberry Pi / microcontrollers, CAD (Fusion 360) & 3D printing, electrical wiring & robot integration (FIRST Robotics), circuit simulation (LTspice), Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, C++

Music: Beat making & sampling, vocals, songwriting & arrangement
