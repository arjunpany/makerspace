Hi, I'm Arjun — Electrical & Computer Engineering is my thing.

It started with taking apart old remotes and radios just to see what was inside, and it hasn't really stopped — now it's Arduino trackers, robot arms, and IoT gardens. What hooked me is that EE/CE sits right at the boundary between physical stuff and code: you design a circuit, write the firmware that drives it, and then watch it actually move, sense, or respond in the real world. This page collects the devices, software, and DIY builds — old and new — that trace that thread. See them laid out chronologically on the timeline.
