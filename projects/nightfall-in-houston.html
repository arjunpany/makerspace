How it was made

This one is inspired by Don Toliver's latest album — specifically the cinematic feel he builds with bells, pads, and leads throughout it. I was really fascinated by how he structures some songs into two distinct parts, so I built this beat the same way, carrying that cinematic quality across both halves.
