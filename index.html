Hi, I'm Arjun — I'm a builder.

It started with a homemade cardboard foosball table and a gaming PC I built at 13, and it hasn't really stopped — now it's competition robots with FIRST Robotics, AI-powered devices, and apps built at hackathons and during an internship. What hooked me is that EE/CE sits right at the boundary between physical stuff and code: you design a circuit, write the firmware that drives it, and then watch it actually move, sense, or respond in the real world. This page collects the devices, software, and DIY builds — old and new — that trace that thread, from a LEGO tribute I built for my grandfather's funeral to the electrical systems on our FRC team's competition robots. See them laid out chronologically on the timeline.
