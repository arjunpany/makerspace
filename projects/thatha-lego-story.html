Thatha Lego Story

A series of handmade LEGO dioramas telling my grandfather's life story, built for his funeral in February 2017.

Timeframe
February 2017
Materials
LEGO minifigures and bricks, custom baseplates, handwritten placards
Category
LEGO, Diorama, Family Tribute

My grandfather — Thatha, which means grandpa in India — passed away in February 2017. To help tell his story at his funeral, I built a series of small LEGO dioramas, each one a scene from his life, laid out in order with a handwritten placard in front of it. It sat on display as "Thatha Lego Story, by Arjun."

The story

Laid out chronologically, the dioramas traced Thatha's life from beginning to end:

  • Thatha was born — 1941
  • Thatha playing cricket — a full pitch scene with a batsman, bowler, and fielders
  • Thatha in college
  • Thatha teaching Physics — Thatha at the front of a classroom with students seated in front of him
  • Thatha enjoying retirement

Each scene was built to capture a specific memory or chapter of his life — the game he loved, the subject he taught, the years after he retired — so that anyone walking past the table could follow his life from start to finish just by reading the placards and looking at the scenes.

How it was built

Each diorama used LEGO minifigures, bricks, and baseplates to recreate the scene, sized up or down as needed — a full pitch for the cricket scene, a single figure at a podium for college, a classroom setup with a blackboard and rows of students for the physics scene. Every scene was paired with a small handwritten placard captioning what it showed, and the whole series was laid out in a row so it could be read left to right like a timeline.

It was a way of using something I already loved building with to tell a story that mattered — a small tribute built by hand for someone who meant a lot to me.
